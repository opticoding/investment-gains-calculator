How it works

See how your money could grow over time.

  • Choose how much to invest and for how many years.
  • Annual gain is how much your money grows in a year. A 10% gain would turn 1,000 into 1,100 after one year.
  • Add regular deposits to put more money in, or withdrawals to take money out.
  • Change the deposit and withdrawal interval and start offset as needed.
  • Gain based shows how your money could grow. Goal based shows the yearly gain you would need to reach a target amount.

The chart is an estimate. Real investments can go up or down.
